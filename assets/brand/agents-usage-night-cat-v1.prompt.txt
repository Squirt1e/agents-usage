Generation tool: built-in image_gen
Design: Night Watch Cat / 夜巡猫

Use case: logo-brand.
Create a single polished square application icon for "agents-usage", a local-first dashboard for monitoring AI agent subscription quotas, remaining balances, and usage. The user wants a dark, technological, abstract cat icon. This project uses near-black #080a0d, charcoal #111419, acid lime #c8ff4d and mint aqua #77e6d8.
Design direction: "Night Watch Cat", calm, intelligent, quietly watchful. A distinctive, extremely simple geometric cat head with two crisp ears and a softly angular lower face, integrated as one coherent emblem. Two minimal luminous horizontal eyes suggest quota indicator bars; one has a tiny dark interruption suggesting remaining capacity. Avoid aggressive slanted eyes. Prefer a confident sculptural silhouette and clever negative space over complex linework. Cat face in cool dark graphite with restrained mint edge highlights, lime eyes as the strong focal point. The silhouette must clearly separate from the very dark background even at small sizes.
Composition: exactly one centered icon, front facing, large symbol occupying roughly 70% of square, generous consistent margins. Near-black rounded-square app tile filling the square canvas with very subtle tonal depth. Premium minimal developer-tool identity, precise geometry, mostly flat vector-like shapes, restrained satin finish and almost imperceptible glow. Bold enough to read at 32 px, beautiful at 1024 px. The main palette remains dark, small lime/mint accents only.
No text, letters, numbers, labels, typography, watermark, chart outside the cat, orbit rings, circuitry clutter, whisker hairlines, robot helmet, gaming esports crest, fangs, photorealistic fur, elaborate background, mockup scene, or multiple variants. Deliver the icon itself as a 1024x1024 square image.
